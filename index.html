Data Analysis:
Where Are You Most Likely To See A Ghost?

All 50 states plus DC, ranked on five haunting factors (ghost sightings, cemeteries, ghost towns, ghost searches and haunted locations), each measured per 100,000 residents. Tap any state for the full numbers.

    How the ranking works: each factor is converted to a rate per 100,000 residents. In every category the 51 states are ranked, earning 51 points for the highest rate down to 1 point for the lowest. The five category scores are added together (maximum 255) to give the final ranking. Tied totals share a rank.

    Sources: FindAGrave, GhostsOfAmerica, Google Keyword Planner, FrightFind, Wikipedia’s lists of ghost towns in the United States. Ghost searches are average monthly Google searches for terms such as “haunted house near me,” “how to get rid of ghosts” and “ghosts.”
